🔒 Chess Escape Room Challenge 🔒

You find yourself in a shadowy library where chess pieces whisper secrets. To escape, you must uncover the number hidden in their power.
Riddles reveal the strength of each piece:
  • Small soldiers march forward, only one step at a time.
  • Jumping knights leap in L-shaped bounds, thrice as strong.
  • Diagonal watchers glide silently, thrice as strong.
  • Corner towers stand firm, five each.
  • The queen reigns supreme, nine in might.
  • The king cannot fall; his value is none.

Solve the librarian's riddle to find the escape number: "Double the strength of the light army, add the heft of the dark, but trim a fraction from their shadows."
